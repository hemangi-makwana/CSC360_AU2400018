## What I Learned

Today I have learned the topics related to the Vol. 2 book, as well as some additional concepts related to graphics. I have learned the difference between downloading and streaming and how streaming helps to address the issue of huge graphics data, such as videos and games.

We also discussed the concepts of the population mean μ, the sample mean x̄, and variance, and how we can use streaming to calculate variance for a big dataset that does not fit in memory.

I have also learned the definition of XML and how it is used in combination with SVG to describe vector graphics with shapes, lines, and paths. We have also talked about how networks can be used to transmit graphic data and how databases can store such data. We have also briefly discussed relational databases and server-side data processing.

----------------

In general, today I have understood that graphics are a large field that intersects with many other fields, such as data processing, storage, networking, and streaming, and is not just a drawing or rendering of images.
